# Fideles, Vol. 2: Laws of Nature and God's Word for Creation
- **Natural Science** - Aspects of created reality which don't involve human activities (such as mathematics or chemistry)
- There are many scientific laws across the fields of natural science, even if they show themselves in different ways.
	- Ex. Biology's laws of genetic inheritance vs. Physic's laws of quantum mechanics
- Four purposes for natural scientific laws:
	1. Classification of phenomena according to laws relevant to them.
	2. Laws are used in the explanation of natural structures and processes.
	3. Used to predict what would reliably happen in given circumstances.
	4. The development of cultural artefacts for specific ends.
- There are several habits of mind that have developed with the advancement of the natural sciences.
	1. Laws of nature are discoverable
	2. Laws of nature ought to be simple
		- Ex. Occam's Razor
	3. Laws are verifiable
	4. Laws are reliable
	5. Laws of nature are causal
		- One thing affects the next
	6. Laws are prescriptive
		- They not only describe the world, but they in fact make the world
	7. The laws of nature are ultimate
		- In some sense, they determine every single thing about the world
- How can a theist reconcile a purely causal universe?
	- It seems that, at a very low level, quantum physics and chaos theory show that there is indeterminism in the functioning of the world.
- The laws of physics are descriptive
- We have gone from Aristotelian physics to Galileo, Newton, and modern Einstein's theory
	- Aristotle: World is made up of elements (Earth, water, air, fire)
	- Galileo: Realized acceleration is constant in a time interval.
	- Newton: Force of gravity is responsible for the constant acceleration of a free-falling body.
	- Quantum field theory understands action at a distance as being due to the exchange of particles (mediating bosons) of a pervasive field
- This is all in the search of the *theory of everything*
- **Verisimilitudinous** describes the laws of physics well; that they increasingly resemble the way things truly are in the universe.
	- We don't totally understand why it is the case that a scientific process in one place is replicated in another. If I conduct an experiment in my lab, we can extrapolate that to assume that those results are true everywhere on earth.

- Jeremiah 33:25 - the *fixed patterns* of heaven and earth
- Genesis 8:22 - God implements and promises a set of regularities which we can rely upon.
