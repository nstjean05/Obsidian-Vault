2 - If a machine passes Turing’s test, claiming for itself self-awareness, does that mean that it is self-aware? How can one tell? Or, does it matter if one cannot?
	Passing the Turing test is neither necessary nor sufficient for self awareness. One can image something that is aware of itself, but could not be able to convince you of its humanity. For instance, my dog seems somewhat self-aware, knowing whether she has done something bad. However, my little daschund could never convince me that she is human. On the other end of the spectrum, many modern AI models pass the Turing test, and with robotics in the future will also become Turing complete. However, that does not mean they are truly self-aware - they simply are excellent at replicating it.
