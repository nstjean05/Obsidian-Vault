# History and Technology
## The Art and Science of History
- Three concepts of history:
	1. History
	2. Technology
	3. Ethics
- Study allows us to look back and project onto the future
#### What does a historian do?
- It is very challenging to create an accurate account of a historical event, even when the given event just occurred.
- We often have challenges when it comes to interpreting the Bible
	- Speculation on contextual particulars without hard evidence
	- Insertion of modern theories, values, and beliefs in the context
- History seeks to explain societal and individual experiences and to integrate opinions,
	- Motivations
	- Causes
	- Actions
	- Reactions
	- Effects
#### Interpreting History
- Historians must also produce a narrative to suitable convey a comprehensive picture
- Evidence, no matter its weight, is irrelevant to people with a sufficiently biased view
- In some worldviews (ex. Marxism), the worldview creates history, and the historical account must conform to the worldview
- Some historical figures once deemed great are now vilified for their social views
- At some points in recent history (pre-2000) Christian politicians had effective runs in office.
	- More recently, as the Christian voting populace has moved to further right-wing nationalism, and erased much of the goodwill between religious and non-religious people
