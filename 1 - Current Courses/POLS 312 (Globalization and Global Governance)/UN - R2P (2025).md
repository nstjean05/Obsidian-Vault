# Global Context and Emerging Patterns of Atrocity Crimes
- Rising occurrence of atrocity crimes
#### Historic Number of Global Conflicts Characterized by Atrocity Crimes
- Fundamental rights and freedoms are being eroded
	- More repression based on national, ethnic, racial, and religious identity
- Countries such as
	- DRC, Ethiopia, Israel and Palestine, Myanmar, Sudan, Ukraine, Yemen
- Despite clear violations, some UN members continue to financially and militarily support the violating nations
- UN Security Council (UNSC) paralysis due to high veto frequency
#### Humanitarian Impact of Conflict
- Large scale conflicts in many regions
	- Great Lakes
	- Lake Chad Basin
	- Sahel
	- Sudan
	- Ukraine
	- Myanmar
- Destruction of civilian infrastructure leading to mass displacement
	- 37 million in 2005 to 123 million displaced in 2024
- Perpetrators frequently sidestep accountability
#### Access to Humanitarian Assistance
- UNSC has condemned use of starvation
	- OPT (Palestine)
	- North Darfur (SUDAN)
	- Syria
	- Yemen
- There have also been deliberate attacks on medical facilities and personnel protected under International Humanitarian Law (IHL)
#### Use of Explosive Weapons and New Tech In Populated Areas
- Methods which show a blatant disregard for human life
- Explosive weapons in populated areas
	- Ex. Bombardments, missile strikes, IEDs
- Expanding use of AI weapons testing
- Lowering the threshold for use of force, amplifying the scale of atrocity
#### Atrocity Crimes in non-Armed Contexts
- Deeply rooted injustices
- Heightened in contexts facing governance breakdown, political instability, democratic backsliding
- Increased risk of identity-based atrocity crimes
# Fulfilling the Objectives of the R2P
#### National Prevention Strategies
- More than a dozen member states have created national mechanisms of prevention
	- Built on Great Lakes Region initiatives, AU foundations, International Commission on Intervention and State Sovereignty
	- R2P principles
- *New Agenda for Peace* emphasizes states' responsibility for conflict prevention and peace building
- Transnational justice is increasingly used to address the aftermath of atrocity crimes
	- Peace agreements in Colombia, Nepal, Philippines, Timor-Leste
#### Regional Initiatives
- Regional organizations play a vital role in implementing R2P
- Ex. Gambia post-election crisis (2016-17)
	- Yahya Jammeh rejecting election results, not stepping down
	- Adama Barrow (incumbent) sworn in at Gambia's Senegalese embassy
	- Senegalese forces entered Gambia to enforce his presidency
	- 6 weeks of tension
	- AU and Economic Community of West African States helped to prevent escalation
	- Jammeh stepped down and was exiled
- Regional networks are instrumental in advancing early warning/protection measures
#### Mobilization of the R2P by Member States Through Multilateral Bodies
- Group of Friends of R2P promoting R2P via joint statements and resolutions across UN bodies
- International Court of Justice hearing genocide cases
	- ICC investigating war crimes
- Increasing reference to R2P in resolutions, building a shared action framework
#### Deeper Integration of R2P to Across UN System
- *Framework of Analysis for Atrocity Crimes* help standardize prevention
# R2P: Conceptual Developments
- UN SG and GA annual reports serving as the UN's primary platforms for advancing R2P
- 2009 - 3 pillar framework
	1. Protection responsibilities of the state
	2. International assistance and capacity building
	3. Timely and decisive response
	- Focus on prevention
	- Narrow in scope (4 atrocity crimes) but deep in the range of tools for response
- Fundamental conviction under R2P remains, despite growing challenges
# Conclusion
- R2P proven relevant
- Future work
	1. Permanent national prevention mechanisms
	2. Enhancing regional dialogue
	3. Developing technical guidance for implementing R2P
- Secretary General urges members to participate in General Assembly R2P debate
