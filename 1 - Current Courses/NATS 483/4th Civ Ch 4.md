# Video Lecture - The Information Revolution
### The Debate
- Internet is an information repository
- Who guarantees that it is secure?
- Where is the information located?
### What is Information?
- Raw data is not information
- It must be:
	- Intentionally and intelligently organized
	- Communicability
	- Purposeful
	- Coded information (like DNA) can encode both
### Interaction
- A society will generate it, but it changes society
- Simple technologies might be the most important
	- Often we don't know what to do with advanced tech
- We filter information for our specialized needs
- More complex society - more elaborate info
- Literacy become highly important
- Any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic
### Feedback Loop
- Generation of more information creates more information and more ways to develop it
### Info and Civ
- Progressively a more defining attribute
- Hunter gatherer
- Agricultural - start to need more organization, control of land
- Industrial - banking to store wealth at scale
- Information Age
### Transmission
- Courier
- Signal towers
	- Started to disrupt stock markets
- Telegraph, fax
- Telephone (POTS, operators, automations)
	- Wire, radio, satellite, cell network, fibre
- Teletype
	- Punched paper tape, 5 or 7 hole codes (ASCII) to dial much faster
- Television and movies
- Internet
