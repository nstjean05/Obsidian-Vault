## Intro
- Complex modern issues are cross-border
- Regional organizations can use combined resources, knowledge, and a unified front to address global challenges.
## Historical Context
#### Rise of Nation States
- Treaty of Westphalia ended the Thirty Years' Ware in 1648
	- Gives rise to modern nation-state
- WWI/II and decolonization reinforce nation states
- Strong ability to adapt to changes
- States are the fundamental actors in international relations
#### Evolution of Regionalism
- Modern idea of regionalism was forged in the 20/21st centuries
- European Coal and Steel Community (1951) preceded the EU
- Spike in regional integration post-1945
## Theoretical Framework of Regionalism
#### Realist
- Regionalism motivated by states strategic interests
	- Maximize power/security
- Ex. NATO keeping North Atlantic region stable
- Deter foreign attack
- Sense of security amongst members
#### Liberal
- Importance of institutions and collaboration internationally
- Encourage economic interdependence
- Political cooperation
- Social integration
- Ex. EU
	- High levels of integration/cooperation/cohesion
	- Single market/customs/currency
	- Significant economic stability and growth
#### Constructivist
- Identities/norms/ideas determine regionalism
- Socially constructed identities and values between states
- Ex. ASEAN
	- Censuses
	- Non-interference norm
- Regional unity and mutual respect
- Common identities
## Power of Regional Collaboration
#### Economic Integration
- The EU's single market has provided an economic model for other regions
	- NAFTA
	- Mercosur
	- AfCFTA
- ASEAN aspires to turn into a unified market and production base
	- Increased investor appeal and global competitiveness
#### United Fronts
- Stronger bargaining positions
- African Union has an important role in African peace/security
	- Deploys peacekeeping missions
	- Mediate crises
	- AU's combined voice can be more successful at advocating for Africa on the global stage
#### Addressing Transnational Issues
- EU collective climate initiatives seek to make Europe the first climate-neutral continent by 2050
- AU efforts to address health difficulties
	- Ebola in West Africa
- Great value in regional collaboration
#### Knowledge Sharing
- Andean Community of Nations (CAN)
	- Collaborate on agriculture, education, and healthcare
#### Regional Stability and Security
- Organisation for Security and Cooperation in Europe
	- OSCE
	- Conflict prevention, crisis management
	- Post-ware reconstruction
#### A Broader Agenda
- Economic integration isn't the only driving force
	- Also includes larger social, cultural, and political goals
- Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC)
	- Collaborate on socials and cultural issues
	- Feeling of common identity
## Pitfalls of Closed Regionalism
- Regionalism can become a source of division when embraced with self-interest
- Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO)
	- Dominating Chinese influence
	- Hindering other states from pursuing autonomous foreign policy
