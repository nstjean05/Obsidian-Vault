# Fideles, Vol. 2: Laws of Nature and God's Word for Creation
- **Natural Science** - Aspects of created reality which don't involve human activities (such as mathematics or chemistry)
- There are many scientific laws across the fields of natural science, even if they show themselves in different ways.
	- Ex. Biology's laws of genetic inheritance vs. Physic's laws of quantum mechanics
- Four purposes for natural scientific laws:
	1. Classification of phenomena according to laws relevant to them.
	2. Laws are used in the explanation of natural structures and processes.
	3. Used to predict what would reliably happen in given circumstances.
	4. The development of cultural artefacts for specific ends.
